Increasing numbers of these organizations are considering or actively working on multicloud architectures.

        ## Motivations for Multicloud
        Significant overlap exists among the products and services of the largest cloud providers.  At the same time, each provider has unique strengths, weaknesses, and "strike zones" wherein a customer's needs are best matched with a provider's unique capabilities.  However, vigiliance through monitoring, measuring and reporting is vital because the intersection of customer needs and provider capabilities is always evolving.

        <br />
        > _the intersection of customer needs and provider capabilities is always evolving_

        <br />
        Factors that influence adoption of a multicloud strategy include:
        * Merger & Acquisition (M&A) activity
        * Provider incentives (credits, discounts, training)
        * Customer-specific requirements of
            * Workloads
            * Support needs
            * Corporate mandates and policies
            
        Despite these mitigations, for any number of reasons, regardless of justification, some organizations may still choose multicloud.
