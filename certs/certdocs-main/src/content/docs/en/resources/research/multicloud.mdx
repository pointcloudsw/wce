Leaders in organizations that run some or all of their business on the cloud evaluate the progress of their cloud journey in terms of cost savings, cost avoidance, time to market for new products/services and number of workloads migrated.  Increasing numbers of these organizations are considering or actively working on multicloud architectures.

## Current challenges
Common challenges encountered by organizations embarking on a cloud journey include the following:
