### Overview
Wonderful though it is, Astro's Starlight doc system has a few annoying defaults, especially as it relates to its right side panel.  By default, the toc on every Starlight page's right sidebar is entitled with the 'On this page', or whatever is your local i18 equivalent (more on this later), heading text.  The second line is even less helpful, with the generic 'Overview' navigation tag existing to quickly navigate the readerto the top of the page.

In this article, I will explain how to override these defaults first with a brute force method which generally works ok and second with a more elegant method that should work, but doesn't, for reasons I will explain.  So let's get started, shall we?
